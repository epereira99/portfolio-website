# Townscaper Art Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the stand-in blocks with the real look from spec §7: Townscaper-like soft, rounded, pastel cities. Each city has its own palette and light, and each has recognizable landmarks: the Arcada, Bom Jesus and the Universidade do Minho in Braga; the Dom Luís I bridge, Clérigos and the Ribeira in Porto; red cottages, pines, snow, Lake Vättern and Jönköping University in Jönköping; Gamla Stan and the City Hall tower in Stockholm. Water appears only where it is real, and the site stays light enough for a recruiter's laptop.

**Architecture:**
- **Ordinary buildings, trees and lamps are generated in code.** Pure functions turn a seeded layout into house and prop specs, and a geometry builder merges each city into one vertex-coloured mesh, so a whole city costs about one draw call.
- **Hero landmarks are Blender-scripted glbs** (same pipeline as the avatar and the nau). They're placed from one data table and loaded per stage.
- **A pure `lightingAt(s)` blends each city's sky, fog, sun and ambient light** as you walk between cities.
- **Water is a small animated shader** on shapes generated per city.

The stand-in modules are deleted at the end.

**Tech Stack:** As before: three 0.187.0, using `RoundedBoxGeometry` and `BufferGeometryUtils.mergeGeometries` from `three/examples/jsm`; R3F 9.8.2; drei 10.7.9; Vitest; Playwright; Blender 5.2.2. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-portfolio-website-design.md` (§6 stages table, §7, §9, §10)

**Builds on:** `SEGMENTS`, `segmentStart`, `segmentAt` (timeline); `pathPoint`, `pathTangent`, `pathPointExtended`, `cameraPose`, `UNITS_PER_SCREEN`, `QUAY_END` (path); `cameraPoseAt`, `nauPose`, `MOOR_OFFSET`, `HULL_HALF_WIDTH` (voyage poses); `standInBlocks`, its water rule and the `StandInCities` component (to be replaced).

## Global Constraints

- **Style (spec §7):** soft, rounded, toy-like low-poly; chunky houses with slightly bulging walls; pastel palettes; soft light and gentle shadows; light distance haze. Cheerful, not childish. No neon or wireframe "AI" look (spec §2).
- **City moods (spec §6):**
  - Braga: morning, warm granite and cream, **no water**.
  - Porto: golden hour, orange roofs, blue-and-white azulejo façades, the Douro.
  - Jönköping: winter blue, red cottages, pines, snow, Lake Vättern.
  - Stockholm: evening, lights on the water, Gamla Stan façades.
- **Water only where it is real (spec §7):** the Douro (Porto, the walker's right), the voyage sea, Lake Vättern (Jönköping, the walker's left), and Stockholm's water (the walker's right). The nau must stay on Porto's water, and the welcome banner and the quay stay on dry ground.
- **Nothing on the walkway:** keep a 3-unit clear corridor from the path centre, and nothing between the camera and the avatar.
- **Performance (spec §10):** at any scroll position, ≤ 120 draw calls and ≤ 600k triangles. Every landmark glb is ≤ 400 KB. Landmarks load for the current and next segment only.
- **Reduced motion:** water waves freeze.
- **The voyage, the ending, the avatar, the nau and all the UI stay unchanged in behaviour.**

## Review Focus

1. **The camera's view of the avatar is never blocked** on the path's bends by a house, tree or landmark. Test in Task 3.
2. **No light "flash" when crossing between cities:** sky, fog and sun change smoothly over the crossing. Test in Task 1.
3. **Jumping straight to Stockholm from the chapter bar:** its landmarks appear without waiting for earlier cities, and a landmark that fails to load leaves the rest of the city standing. Tests in Task 7.
4. **A recruiter's laptop:** the draw-call and triangle budget holds at every city. Test in Task 9.
5. **Reduced motion:** the water stops moving. Test in Task 5.

### Task 4: Merged city geometry and the CityArt component

**Files:**
- Create: `src/journey/art/buildCity.ts`, `src/journey/art/buildCity.test.ts`, `src/journey/art/CityArt.tsx`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `cityLayout` (Task 3), `CITY_STYLE` (Task 1).
- Produces:
  ```ts
  export function buildCityGeometry(layout: CityLayout, id: SegmentId): BufferGeometry; // one merged, vertex-coloured geometry
  export function CityArt(): JSX.Element;   // one <mesh> per city: meshStandardMaterial({ vertexColors: true, roughness: 0.85 }), castShadow, receiveShadow
  ```
- **House geometry:**
  - The body is a `RoundedBoxGeometry` (radius 0.18, 3 segments) scaled to w × floors·floorHeight × d.
  - The roof depends on its kind:
    - gable: a triangular prism, pitch 35°, eaves 0.12;
    - hip: a 4-sided pyramid frustum;
    - stepped: three stacked, narrowing boxes on the front gable;
    - flat: a thin slab.
  - Windows are rounded dark insets (`#3b4152`, slightly lighter in Stockholm with an emissive-looking warm `#ffd28a` via vertex colour on about 40% of windows), 2 per floor on the front face, plus a door on the ground floor.
  - Azulejo houses get a pattern of blue-and-white tiles on the front face: a grid of small slightly-raised squares alternating `#2f5fa8` and `#f4f7fb`.
- **Trees:**
  - round: a sphere crown on a short trunk;
  - pine: three stacked cones, with white cone tips when `snow` is true.
- **Lamps:** a thin post with a ball top, glowing `#ffd28a` in Stockholm.
